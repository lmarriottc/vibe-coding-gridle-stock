# FormaStock scope

## Included

- English-language sales and inventory dashboard.
- Sales and purchase movements by model and size.
- Shared Google Sheets persistence through Google Apps Script.
- Inventory totals, daily sales, low-stock indicators, search, and recent history.
- Local demonstration mode when a Sheet is not configured.

## Excluded for now

- Application-managed users and passwords.
- Payments, invoicing, and accounting integrations.
- Advanced product catalog administration.
- Offline conflict resolution.
